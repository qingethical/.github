## What is QEHG?

**Qings Ethical Hacking Group**, known as **QEHG**, is an ethical hacking and cybersecurity group focused on making security knowledge easier to understand.

QEHG was originally created to teach the **basics of ethical hacking**, security concepts, programming, and practical cybersecurity techniques.

> **Hack to learn. Learn to protect.**

We believe cybersecurity should not be mysterious or locked behind walls of complicated terminology.

## Our Mission

QEHG exists to help people understand cybersecurity **responsibly**.

Our goals are simple:

*  Make cybersecurity easier to learn
*  Encourage curiosity and experimentation
*  Teach useful programming skills
*  Promote secure development
*  Encourage responsible security research
*  Build a community around learning

No unnecessary gatekeeping.

No pretending that cybersecurity is some secret wizard discipline.

Just **learn, experiment, understand, and build.**
